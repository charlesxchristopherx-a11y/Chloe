The Chloe Nicole's Approach

A calm plan. A generous table. A gathering that feels like you.

01

Share your vision

Tell us about your gathering — the occasion, the people, the feel you're after. No formality, no pressure. Just a conversation about what matters to you.

02

Shape the experience

Together we build a menu and plan that fits your people, your space, and your budget. Every detail gets attention — dietary needs, service style, timeline.

03

Confirm with confidence

A clear quote, a simple contract, and a 50% deposit locks in your date. Balance due eight days before. No hidden fees, no last-minute surprises.

04

Gather & enjoy

We arrive, set up, and handle everything — so you can be fully present with your people. Hot food, warm service, and a table that brings everyone together.

Yes — professional wait staff and setup/cleanup crew are included with most packages. For drop-off catering, staff isn't required but can be added. Every quote spells out staffing so there are no surprises.

We're based in Pittsburgh and serve the greater Western Pennsylvania region. No hard travel limit — if your venue is within a reasonable drive, we'll be there. Travel fees are quoted upfront.

A 50% deposit secures your date. The balance is due eight days before the event. Cancellations more than 14 days out receive a full refund of the deposit. Within 14 days, the deposit is non-refundable but can be applied toward a rescheduled date.

Our menus typically start at $18–22/person for casual gatherings and range to $45+/person for full-service wedding packages. Every event gets a custom quote based on your guest count, menu, and service needs. No hidden fees — everything is spelled out upfront.
