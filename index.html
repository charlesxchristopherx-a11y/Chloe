Good to know

Questions before the first conversation.

Have something different in mind? Include it in your event notes.

Is there a catering minimum?+

The standard catering minimum is 25 guests. Events below 25 guests add $5.50 per person.

Can the menu be customized?+

Yes. The menu collection is a starting point, and options are available for dietary needs including vegan, gluten-free, and vegetarian requests. Final selections are confirmed during planning.

Why aren't prices listed on the menus?+

Food costs change frequently, so menu prices are prepared as custom quotes rather than posted online. Your quote will reflect the selected dishes, guest count, service style, and event details.

Are setup, cleanup, and wait staff available?+

Yes. Professional wait staff are available, food is delivered hot, and setup and cleanup can be included. Your proposal will state the service planned for your event.

How are dates and payments confirmed?+

A 50% deposit is required within three days of booking to hold the date. Final payment is due eight days before the event, and the final guest count is due two weeks before the event. Credit cards, checks, and cash are accepted.

How far do you travel, and is there a delivery fee?+

There is no fixed travel-distance limit. Availability is confirmed for each event, and the delivery and setup fee is generally $60 to $100 based on the location and setup needs.

What are your business hours?+

Because events and planning needs vary, Chloe Nicole's does not keep fixed office hours. Quote requests can be made by text, email, or phone call.

What if the event plans change?+

Changes or cancellations made within two weeks of the event carry a 20% fee. Approved refunds are mailed within 30 days. Your signed proposal will contain the controlling terms for your booking.

Do you cater on Sundays?+

Chloe Nicole's Catering is closed on Sundays and does not offer Sunday catering service.
